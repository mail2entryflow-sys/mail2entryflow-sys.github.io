How to use / എങ്ങനെ ഉപയോഗിക്കാം

◎ My location – shows where you are and the survey number you are standing in. The strip at the top keeps showing your survey number as you walk.
എന്റെ സ്ഥാനം: നിങ്ങൾ നിൽക്കുന്ന സർവ്വേ നമ്പർ കാണിക്കും.

Tap the map – survey number, village, block, district and the approximate area of the whole survey number, outlined in yellow.
മാപ്പിൽ തൊടുക: സർവ്വേ നമ്പർ, വില്ലേജ്, വിസ്തീർണ്ണം.

📄 FMB sketch – on a survey number's window: load a photo, scan or PDF of its FMB sketch, drag a box around the drawing, and it is laid over the yellow outline. Then Match corners: tap a corner on the sketch, then the same corner on the yellow outline – 2 to 4 corners give a close fit, showing the sub-divisions on the ground. Kept only on this phone.

📐 Align survey lines (🗺️ Layers) – Bhuvan's lines are often shifted from the real ground. Tap a corner of the orange lines, then the same corner on the satellite picture; 2–3 corners spread around correct the whole village. The correction is used for every survey number you look up there. Share it so other investigators get it too.

⭐ Save plot – keep your cluster's plots on this phone, with a note. They stay on the map in green.

📏 Measure – tap points to measure a distance; three or more points also give the area (cents).

⭕ Circle – 0.5 to 5 km around you. 📂 Load – show a ward boundary (KML/GeoJSON).

🗺️ Layers – satellite or street map, survey and village layers.

Survey numbers and boundaries come from Bhuvan Panchayat (NRSC/ISRO) and may be old where resurvey was done. GPS is accurate to about 5–10 m. Village records are final. Your location is used only to look up the survey number; saved plots stay on this phone.
